# VitalZen AI Friction Log

Date: 2026-05-21

## 1) First-run uncertainty
New users may not immediately understand how scan quality affects metric reliability.

## 2) Permissions confidence gap
Camera and notification prompts may feel abrupt without in-context rationale.

## 3) Metric interpretation load
Heart rate, stress, and wellness scores appear quickly but can lack plain-language interpretation.

## 4) Navigation discoverability
Some users may miss where trend analytics and account actions live.

## 5) Trust and transparency
Users can want a clearer explanation of where data is stored, synced, and deleted.

## 6) Session continuity
Returning users may not have a clear next-best action after opening the app.

## 7) Error recovery
If scan capture quality drops, corrective steps should be immediate and specific.

## 8) International readiness
Language options exist, but users may expect localized legal/help content as well.
